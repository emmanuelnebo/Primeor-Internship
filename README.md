# E-Commerce Sales & Business Performance Analysis | Primeor Solutions Internship Project
### An End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Primeor Solutions Internship 

**Author:** Emmanuel Chimaobi Nebo  
**Project:** E-Commerce Data Analytics  
**Organisation:** Primeor Solutions  
**Dataset Period:** 2011–2014  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | Power Query | SQL | Power BI | DAX

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing historical e-commerce transactional data to evaluate business performance, identify sales and profitability trends, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and uncover opportunities for business growth.

Working with a dataset containing **51,257 records**, I carried out an end-to-end analytical process involving data inspection, cleaning, transformation, SQL-based analysis, and interactive dashboard development using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that could support strategic decision-making, improve operational visibility, and drive business performance.

## 2. Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions:

- What is the overall sales and profitability performance of the business?
- How have sales and profit evolved over time?
- Which product categories and sub-categories contribute most to revenue and profit?
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
- Which markets and regions demonstrate stronger business performance?
- Who are the top-performing customers?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- What areas of the business require attention to improve profitability?

## 4. Data Preparation & Transformation

Data quality was an important part of this project. Before performing the analysis, the dataset was inspected and prepared to improve consistency and reliability.

Key activities included:

- Inspecting the dataset structure and identifying data quality issues.
- Identifying and removing duplicate records.
- Replacing missing customer names with `Unknown Customer` to preserve transaction records.
- Removing records with missing profit values.
- Cleaning and transforming data using Power Query.
- Preparing the cleaned dataset for SQL analysis and Power BI reporting.

## 5. Analytical Approach

The project followed a structured analytical workflow:

**Step 1 – Data Inspection:**  
Explored the raw dataset in Microsoft Excel to understand its structure, fields, and quality.

**Step 2 – Data Cleaning:**  
Used Power Query to address duplicates, missing values, and inconsistencies.

**Step 3 – SQL Analysis:**  
Applied SQL queries to explore business performance, aggregate metrics, and investigate key business questions.

**Step 4 – Data Modelling & DAX:**  
Developed the Power BI data model, created analytical measures, and calculated business KPIs.

**Step 5 – Dashboard Development:**  
Designed an interactive, three-page Power BI dashboard to communicate findings through visualisations and performance indicators.

## 8. Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important findings:

- **Customer Segment Performance:** The Consumer segment generated the largest share of sales, contributing approximately 51.46% of total revenue.
- **Product Category Performance:** Technology recorded the highest sales among the three major product categories.
- **Profitability:** Chairs were among the strongest profit-generating sub-categories, while Tables recorded a negative profit contribution.
- **Customer Performance:** Customer-level analysis identified the highest-value customers and provided visibility into customer revenue contribution.
- **Product Performance:** Product-level analysis highlighted top-selling products and areas of stronger sales performance.

## 9. Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Improve Profitability:** Investigate loss-making product sub-categories and review their pricing, discounting, and cost structures.
2. **Optimise Discount Strategies:** Evaluate the impact of discounts on sales volume and profit margins.
3. **Strengthen Customer Relationships:** Develop retention strategies around valuable customer segments and high-performing customers.
4. **Prioritise High-Performing Products:** Leverage insights from top-selling products to inform inventory and sales strategies.
5. **Monitor Regional Performance:** Use market and geographical analysis to identify growth opportunities and areas requiring improvement.
